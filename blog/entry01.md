I started trying to figure out how to use GoDot and find some videos. I found a [video about a person trying to make a desktop pet](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x8BO9C6YtlE&t=67s). I went over to the [official GoDOt](https://godotengine.org/download/linux/) to download it. It did not work like I intended it to be. I later found this [GoDot Engine](https://editor.godotengine.org/releases/latest/), but I still can't incorporate it into my IDE. So I asked Mr. Mueller about how to do that.
