### Presenting my Project
I had finished creating my desktop pet and had started on my presentation. First, I started off with my [plan](https://github.com/xinyangl5722/apcsa-freedom-project/blob/main/prep/presentation.md) in order to help me get an outline of my presentation. I later started putting up the information for the presentation. In the end, the presentation looks like [this](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1anV4hEveE3GM_ID9nX_YnFPeAUllehp-AB-izRimnOw/edit). 

As I presented in class, sometimes I forgot most of the information I was supposed to present. Sometimes I lagged my words a little bit because I don't know what to say next. I learned that I had to be more prepared for giving the presentation as I had time to prepare but I didn't. 

My presentation at the expo (exposition) was not so bad. I was one of the last ones to present to the judges, but when it was my turn to present, I went through the sequence I had prepared since the beginning of the expo. I first went on to the demonstration, then the key points of my process. I also acted enthusiastically about my project to make it sound more interesting and impressive to the judges. After one of the judges said that my project looks very cute and looked very happy about it, I learned that acting more enthusiastically about your project will project to the audience and make them think it is cool too.

### EDP
So far, I have finished **communicating the results**. I had presented my project to the class for the in-class presentation, and I had presented it at the SEP (Software Engineering Program) Exposition. I had a few takeaways while doing this project such as _time management_ that I need to use my time wisely and _preparation beforehand_ which I should have prepared days before the day of the presentation.

### Skills
One skill I have developed was **Communication** as I had presented my project in class and at the expo where I learned to maintain eye contact and present my project with enthusiasm. Another skill I had developed was **growth mindset**. Despite having a lot of challenges in my project journeys such as my project not working or not feeling like my project is good enough, I still managed to get to the end and presented my own project to judges and other audiences that approached it.

Now, I finished my project all together from learning the tool, to building the product, and all the way to presenting it. I am looking forward to how all of this will lead me to what I will do in the future.
